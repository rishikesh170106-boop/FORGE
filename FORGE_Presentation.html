⚠️ THE CIVIC GRIDLOCK

Why Modern Municipalities Struggle

Communities face hundreds of civic hazards daily. Existing reporting systems fail across 4 critical failure modes:

1. Opaque Black Box

Citizens file complaints on confusing web portals with zero real-time progress updates, leading to widespread citizen fatigue and distrust.

2. Manual Triage Bottlenecks

Control room staff spend 2 to 5 days manually reading text descriptions just to determine which municipal department holds responsibility.

3. Duplicate Ticket Storms

A single hazardous pothole gets reported by 20 different residents, producing 20 disconnected tickets that overwhelm municipal queues.

4. Fake Closures & Zero Proof

Complaints are marked "Resolved" on paper by contractors without physical photographic verification or citizen confirmation.

🔄 OPERATIONAL TRANSPARENCY

9-Stage Government-to-Citizen Workflow

Transparent 4-role operational hierarchy: Citizen, Department Officer, Field Specialist, and City Administrator.

1. SUBMITTED

Citizen photo uploaded with live geolocation & optional voice notes.

2. AI_ANALYZED

Gemini 3.8 classifies category, severity, and flags safety risks.

3. ROUTED

Jurisdictional Ward & Department mapped with target SLA hours.

4. ACCEPTED

Ward officer claims complaint and initiates municipal work order.

5. ASSIGNED

Work Order generated; specialist field crew dispatched.

6. IN_PROGRESS

On-site crew repairs defect (asphalt patch, pipe replacement, etc.).

7. RESOLUTION_SUBMITTED

Crew uploads "After" photo evidence and completion notes.

8. VERIFIED

Gemini AI Vision + Officer inspects & validates genuine resolution.

9. CLOSED

Citizen confirms satisfactory repair; award citizen gamification points.
